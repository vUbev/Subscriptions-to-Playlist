# Subscriptions-to-Playlist
Automatically adds new videos from channels you choose (seen on your YouTube Subscriptions feed) to a playlist, with a hover button to add or remove channels.

Pick the playlist: A settings page opens when you install the add-on, and when you click its icon. Choose the playlist there. It defaults to Watch Later, I personally have a "background" playlist.

Pick the channels: Add them on the settings page, or directly on YouTube. A button appears over video thumbnails (and next to Subscribe on a video page). It turns green for channels on your list and red for those that aren’t. Click it to add or remove the channel.

How it works: Whenever your YouTube Subscriptions feed is open, the add-on checks the videos shown. If one is from a channel on your list, and isn’t already in your playlist, it adds it. You can skip videos you’ve already watched, and a dry-run mode lets you preview what it would add.

Privacy: Everything is stored locally in your browser. The add-on only talks to YouTube, using your signed-in session, to read and edit your playlist. It sends nothing to the developer or any third party.

Add-on was vibecoded, then tested and modified by a human (me!).
